Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Steel Optimizer (steeloptimizer.com) is a browser-based steel cutting and nesting optimizer operated by Mezher Nada in Saudi Arabia. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, where it is kept, for how long, and your rights under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Contact: support@steeloptimizer.com.

1. Your files stay on your device

Material lists, Excel and CSV files, PDFs and photos you open in Steel Optimizer are read and processed inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. The only exception is a file you choose to email to us for support.

2. What we collect and why

We process this data with your consent (you choose to give your email or create an account) or because it is needed to provide the service you asked for.

3. Payments

Subscriptions are sold by Lemon Squeezy as our Merchant of Record. Your card or wallet details are entered on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page and never reach our site. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy applies to the data it collects.

4. Data kept in your browser

Your browser's local storage keeps your language, your settings, your sign-in session and whether you already gave an email, so you are not asked again. It stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Service providers and transfers outside Saudi Arabia

We use Supabase (database and accounts), Lemon Squeezy (payments), Formspree (the email forms and usage notes), Vercel (hosting and statistics), and Google Fonts, cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr (fonts and the PDF and photo readers; the photo reader also downloads its English language file from tessdata.projectnaptha.com). Like any website, these providers receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads content from them. Their servers may be outside Saudi Arabia. We share only the minimum data needed, and we do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Launch list and download emails: until you ask us to delete them, and no longer than 12 months after the paid launch if you do not create an account.
  • Account and subscription data: while your account is active; deleted within 30 days after you ask us to close it, except records the law requires us to keep.
  • Support emails and files: deleted within 12 months after your request is resolved.
  • Anonymous usage counts and statistics: kept in aggregate; they do not identify you.

When a period ends, the data is deleted.

7. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is processed;
  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or updated;
  • ask us to delete it when it is no longer needed;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

Email support@steeloptimizer.com from the address concerned and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

8. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed by Supabase. Database rules let each signed-in user read only their own subscription. Card details never touch our site.

9. Children

Steel Optimizer is a professional tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the date at the top.

  • بريد قائمة الإطلاق والتنزيل: حتى تطلب حذفه، وبحد أقصى 12 شهراً بعد الإطلاق المدفوع إن لم تُنشئ حساباً.
